# Invoice Details

One customer bill, and the money received against it.

## What you see here
- Customer details and the bill's dates.
- Each item, with shade, quantity, rate and GST.
- Payments already received.

## The money figures
- **Invoice Total** — the full bill including GST.
- **Paid** — what the customer has paid so far.
- **Outstanding** — what they still owe.
- **Overdue** — shown when the due date has passed and money is still owed.

## What you can do
- **Confirm** — on a draft, finishes the bill and reduces your stock.
- **Void** — cancels a confirmed bill. **The goods go back into your stock.**
- **Mark as Paid** — records that the bill has been settled in full.
- Change the **Due Date** if the customer has been given more time.

> Important: **Void** is the correct way to cancel a confirmed bill. Do not
> make a second bill to cancel out the first — that would count the stock
> movement twice.
