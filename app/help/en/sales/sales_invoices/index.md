# Sales Invoices

Every bill you have made for a customer.

## To make a bill
Click **New Invoice** (top-right).

## What the columns mean
- **Invoice #** — the bill number.
- **Customer** — who it is for.
- **Mode** — how they paid or will pay.
- **Items** — how many product lines are on it.
- **Total (₹)** — the full bill including GST.
- **Payment** — whether it is paid, part-paid or unpaid.
- **Due Date** — when payment is expected.

## Status
- **Draft** — not finished. Stock has **not** been reduced.
- **Confirmed** — finished. Stock **has** been reduced.
- **Voided** — cancelled after being confirmed. The stock was put back.

> Tip: a bill sitting in **Draft** has not reduced your stock. If your stock
> looks higher than the shelf, check for unconfirmed drafts here.
