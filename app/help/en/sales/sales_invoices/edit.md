# Edit Sales Invoice

Change a bill that is still a **Draft**.

## What you can change
The customer, the items, shades, quantities, rates and the payment details.
Check the **Grand Total** after any change.

## Saving
Save your changes to keep the bill as a draft. Stock is still not touched.
**Confirm** it when the customer is taking the goods.

## Why some bills cannot be edited
Only **Draft** bills can be changed. Once a bill is **Confirmed**, the stock
has already been reduced.

If a confirmed bill is wrong, open it and use **Void** — that cancels it
properly and returns the goods to stock. Then make a fresh bill.

> Tip: voiding and re-billing is safe. Making a second bill *without* voiding
> the first is not — your stock will be wrong.
