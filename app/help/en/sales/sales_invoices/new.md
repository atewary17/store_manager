# New Sales Invoice

Make a bill for a customer.

## Step 1 — Choose the customer
Start typing the name and pick from the list. For a cash sale with no regular
customer, use **Walk-in Customer**.

## Step 2 — Add what they are buying
Click to add a row for each item:

- **Product** — start typing and pick from your catalogue.
- **Shade** — for paint, either pick a shade from the list or type the shade
  name yourself if it is not there.
- **Qty** — how many units.
- **Rate** — price per unit.
- **GST %** — filled in from the product.

The line total and the **Grand Total** work themselves out.

## Step 3 — Payment details
Set the payment **Mode** and, if the customer is paying later, the
**Due Date**. **Accounts Receivable** uses that date to show overdue bills.

## Step 4 — Review, then confirm
Click **Review Invoice**. The bill is saved as a draft and you are taken to a
preview page.

That page shows each item's **Current Stock**, how much will be **Deduct**ed,
and warns you where stock is **Low**. Check it, then **Confirm**.

Confirming is what **takes the goods out of your stock**.

> Important: nothing leaves your stock until you confirm. Only confirm when the
> customer is actually taking the goods.
