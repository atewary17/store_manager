# Add a Referrer

Record a painter or contractor who sends customers to your shop, so you can
credit sales to them later.

## Fields
- **Name** *(required)* — the person's name.
- **Trade** — painter, contractor, electrician, plumber, carpenter, mason,
  civil or other.
- **Area** — the locality they work in. Useful for finding them later.
- **Phone** — their number. **Each phone number can only be used once**, so if
  the app refuses it, that person is already on the list.

## How referrers are used
When making a sales invoice, you can credit it to a referrer. Their
**Total Value** and **Invoices** on the Referrers list then build up over time.

> Tip: check the list before adding someone new. A referrer added twice splits
> their business across two records and makes the totals wrong.
