# Referrer Details

One referrer, and the business they have brought you.

## What you see
- Their name, trade, area and phone number.
- **Invoices** — how many bills have been credited to them.
- **Total Value** — what that business is worth.
- **Last Referral** — when they last sent a customer.
- The list of bills credited to them.

## To change their details
Use **Edit**.

> Tip: this page is the answer to "how much business has this painter actually
> sent us?" — useful before agreeing a discount or commission.
