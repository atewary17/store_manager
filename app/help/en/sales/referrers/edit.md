# Edit Referrer

Correct a referrer's details.

## Fields
- **Name** *(required)* — the person's name.
- **Trade** — painter, contractor, electrician, plumber, carpenter, mason,
  civil or other.
- **Area** — the locality they work in.
- **Phone** — their number. It must still be unique; if the app refuses it,
  another referrer already has that number.
- **Status** — set to inactive if they no longer send you customers. This keeps
  their past sales history without showing them in new bills.

> Tip: make someone inactive rather than deleting them. Deleting would lose the
> record of business they brought you.
