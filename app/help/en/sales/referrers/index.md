# Referrers

Painters, contractors and tradespeople who send customers to your shop.

## To add one
Click **New Referrer** (top-right).

## What the columns mean
- **Name** — the person's name.
- **Trade** — painter, contractor, electrician, plumber, carpenter, mason,
  civil or other.
- **Area** — the locality they work in.
- **Phone** — their contact number. Each number can only be used once.
- **Invoices** — how many bills have been credited to them.
- **Total Value** — the value of business they have brought in.
- **Last Referral** — when they last sent a customer.
- **Status** — whether they are still active.

> Tip: sort by **Total Value** to see which tradespeople actually bring you the
> most business.
