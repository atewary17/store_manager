# Customer Account

Every unpaid bill for one customer.

## What the columns mean
- **Invoice #** — the bill number.
- **Date** — when the bill was made.
- **Total (₹)** — the full bill including GST.
- **Paid (₹)** — what has been received against it.
- **Outstanding (₹)** — what is still owed on it.
- **Status** — paid, part-paid or overdue.

## To record money received
Go to **Customer Receipts** and record the payment against the bill. The
**Outstanding** figure here updates once it is saved.

A customer can pay part of a bill. The rest stays outstanding.

> Tip: if a bill the customer has already settled still shows as outstanding,
> the receipt has probably not been entered yet.
