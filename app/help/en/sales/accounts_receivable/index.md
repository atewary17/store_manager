# Accounts Receivable

What customers owe you, one row per customer.

## What the columns mean
- **Customer** — who owes the money.
- **Invoices** — how many of their bills still have money outstanding.
- **Invoiced (₹)** — the value of those bills.
- **Collected (₹)** — how much they have paid.
- **Outstanding (₹)** — what is still owed. This is the number that matters.
- **Next Due Date** — the earliest payment still expected. A date in the past
  means that customer has an overdue bill.

Click a customer to see exactly which bills are unpaid.

## Where these numbers come from
Only **Confirmed** bills are counted. Drafts are not money owed, and **Voided**
bills are excluded because they were cancelled.

> Tip: sort by **Next Due Date** to see who to chase first.
