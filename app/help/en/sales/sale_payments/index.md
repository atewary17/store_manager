# Customer Receipts

Every payment you have received from a customer.

## To record a receipt
Open the customer's bill and record the payment against it, entering:

- **Amount (₹)** — how much they paid. This can be part of the bill; the rest
  stays outstanding.
- **Date** — the day you actually received the money.
- **Mode** — cash, cheque, bank transfer, UPI, NEFT or RTGS.
- **Ref No.** — cheque number, UPI reference or transaction ID.

## What the columns mean
- **Receipt** — the app's own number for this payment.
- **Invoice** — the bill it was received against.
- **Recorded by** — which user entered it.

## Filtering
Use the search box and the customer filter at the top to find a particular
receipt.

## What happens after you save
The bill's **Outstanding** drops by the amount received, and so does the
customer's total in **Accounts Receivable**.

> Tip: always fill in **Ref No.** It is the only thing that tells two similar
> payments apart when you check your bank statement.
