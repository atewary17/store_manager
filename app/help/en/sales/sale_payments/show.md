# Receipt Details

One payment received from a customer, opened from the bill it belongs to.

## What you see
- **Date**, **Amount (₹)**, **Mode** and **Ref No.** for this receipt.
- The invoice it was received against, with that bill's total, amount paid and
  outstanding balance.

## To see all receipts together
Open **Customer Receipts** for the full list across all customers.

> Tip: keep the **Ref No.** accurate. At month end it is what lets you match
> this receipt to a line on your bank statement.
