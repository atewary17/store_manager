# Receipt Details

One payment received from a customer, and the bill it was for.

## The receipt
- **Date** — when you received the money.
- **Amount (₹)** — how much this receipt was for.
- **Mode** — cash, cheque, bank transfer, UPI, NEFT or RTGS.
- **Ref No.** — cheque number, UPI reference or transaction ID.

## The bill it belongs to
The invoice number, its total, everything paid against it so far, and what is
still outstanding.

## All payments on this invoice
A bill can be settled in parts. This shows every receipt against it, so you can
see the full history in one place.

> Tip: if **Outstanding** is not what you expect, check this list — a receipt
> may have been entered twice, or against the wrong bill.
