# Sales

This section is for everything you **sell** — customer bills, what customers
owe you, and the money you collect.

## The usual order of work
1. **Sales Invoices → New** — make a bill for the customer.
2. Check it and **Confirm**. This takes the goods out of your stock.
3. **Accounts Receivable** shows what each customer still owes.
4. **Customer Receipts** records the money they pay you.

## What the words mean
- **Draft** — a bill you are still working on. Stock is untouched.
- **Confirmed** — finished. Stock has been reduced.
- **Voided** — a confirmed bill that was cancelled. The stock was put back.
- **Outstanding** — the part of a bill the customer has not paid yet.

> Note: unlike purchase bills, a confirmed sales bill can be **voided** if it
> was wrong. Voiding returns the goods to your stock.
