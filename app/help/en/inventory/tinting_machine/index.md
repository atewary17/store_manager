# Tinting Machine

The colourant canisters in your tinting machine, and how full each one is.

## What you see
One card per brand of machine. Each shows its canister slots and how much
colourant is left in each.

Click a machine to open it and work with the individual slots.

## Why this is tracked
Colourant is stock too. Knowing which canisters are running low means you can
order before you are caught out mid-job.

> Tip: check this at the start of the week, not when a customer is waiting.
