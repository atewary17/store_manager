# Canister Slots

Each slot in this tinting machine, and how much colourant is in it.

## Slot status
- **Empty** — nothing loaded in this slot.
- **Active** — loaded and has plenty left.
- **Low** — under 30% remaining. Time to order more.
- **Critical** — nearly empty. Refill before the next job.

## What you can do
- **Select Product** and **Volume (ml)** — choose which colourant goes into a
  slot and how much is in it, then load it.
- **+** and **−** on a slot — each tap moves **25%** of that canister's full
  volume. Use them as colourant is dispensed.
- **Reload** — refills a slot with the same product it held last time.
- **Remove** — takes the canister out of the slot. You are asked to confirm
  first.

The machine picture can be dragged and scrolled to look around. The **Reset**
button above it only straightens your view — it does not change any canister.

## Keeping it accurate
The machine does not report back to the app, so the figures are only as good as
what is entered. Update a slot whenever you refill it.

> Tip: a canister showing **Low** or **Critical** is your reorder signal.
> Checking weekly avoids running out during a job.
