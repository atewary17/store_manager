# Inventory

This section shows **what you have in the shop** and what it is worth.

## Where stock comes from
You do not usually change stock by hand. It moves on its own:

- A **purchase bill** is confirmed → stock goes **up**.
- A **sales invoice** is confirmed → stock goes **down**.
- A sales invoice is **voided** → stock goes back **up**.
- **Opening Stock** is used once, when you first start using the app.

## What the words mean
- **Qty on Hand** — how many units you have right now.
- **Avg Cost** — the average price you paid per unit.
- **Stock Value** — Qty on Hand × Avg Cost.

> Tip: if a figure looks wrong, the cause is almost always a bill — an
> unconfirmed draft, or something entered twice. Check Purchasing and Sales
> before adjusting stock by hand.
