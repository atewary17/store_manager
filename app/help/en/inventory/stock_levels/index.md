# Stock Levels

What you have in the shop right now, product by product.

## What the columns mean
- **Product**, **Brand**, **Category**, **Material Code** — which item it is.
- **UOM** — the unit it is counted in (litres, pieces and so on).
- **Qty on Hand** — how many units you have now.
- **Avg Cost (₹)** — the average price you have paid per unit. It is worked out
  across all your purchases, so it changes as prices change.
- **Stock Value (₹)** — Qty on Hand × Avg Cost.

## Adjusting stock by hand
The **+** and **−** buttons change the quantity by one. Use them only for real
differences you have counted — breakage, samples, or a shelf count that does
not match.

If the buttons are greyed out, your administrator has locked manual stock
changes.

## Where these numbers come from
Stock moves on its own when bills are confirmed. Purchases add, sales subtract,
voided sales add back.

> Important: do not use **+** and **−** to fix a mistake on a bill. Correct the
> bill instead — otherwise the stock and the paperwork stop agreeing.
