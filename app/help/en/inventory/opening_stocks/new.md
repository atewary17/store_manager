# Opening Stock Entry

Record what you already have on the shelf when you start using the app.

Use this **once**, at the beginning. After that, stock looks after itself as
you confirm purchase and sales bills.

## How to enter it
1. Narrow the list using **Brand** and **Category**, or leave them on
   **All Brands** / **All Categories**.
2. Find the **Product**.
3. Enter the quantity you counted on the shelf.
4. Add the cost per unit if you know it — this becomes the starting
   **Avg Cost** for that product and feeds your stock value.
5. Use **Notes** to say where the figure came from, for example "shelf count
   1 April".

## Getting it right
Count carefully. Everything afterwards is worked out from this starting point,
so a wrong opening figure stays wrong until someone notices.

> Tip: do the count when the shop is closed, and enter it the same day.
