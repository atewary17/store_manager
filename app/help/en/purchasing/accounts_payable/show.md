# Supplier Account

Every unpaid bill for one supplier.

## What the columns mean
- **Invoice #** — the supplier's bill number.
- **Date** — the date on the bill.
- **Due Date** — when payment is expected. A date in the past means overdue.
- **Total (₹)** — the full bill including GST.
- **Paid (₹)** — what you have paid against that bill.
- **Outstanding (₹)** — what is still owed on it.
- **Status** — whether it is paid, part-paid or overdue.

## To pay a bill
Go to **Supplier Payments** and record the payment against the bill. The
**Outstanding** figure here updates once it is saved.

You can pay part of a bill. The rest stays outstanding until you pay it.

> Tip: if a bill you have already paid still shows as outstanding, the payment
> has probably not been recorded in the app yet.
