# Accounts Payable

What you owe, supplier by supplier. One row per supplier.

## What the columns mean
- **Supplier** — who you owe money to.
- **Invoices** — how many of their bills still have money owing.
- **Total Invoiced (₹)** — the value of those bills.
- **Paid (₹)** — how much you have paid against them.
- **Outstanding (₹)** — what is still owed. This is the number that matters.
- **Next Due Date** — the earliest payment date still coming up. If it is in
  the past, that supplier has an overdue bill.

Click a supplier to see exactly which bills are unpaid.

## Where these numbers come from
Only **Confirmed** bills appear here. A bill still in **Draft** is not counted
as money owed, because it is not finished yet.

> Tip: sort by **Next Due Date** to see who needs paying first.
