# Purchasing

This section is for everything you **buy** — supplier bills, what you owe, and
the payments you make.

## The usual order of work
1. **Scan Invoice** — take a photo of a paper bill and let the app read it.
   Or use **Purchase Bills → New** to type one in yourself.
2. Check the details and **Confirm** the bill. This adds the goods to your stock.
3. **Accounts Payable** shows what is still owed to each supplier.
4. **Supplier Payments** records the money you pay them.

## What the words mean
- **Draft** — a bill you are still working on. Nothing has been added to stock.
- **Confirmed** — you have checked the bill and saved it. Stock is updated.
- **Outstanding** — the part of a bill you have not paid yet.

> Important: once a bill is **Confirmed**, it cannot be changed. Check the
> quantity, rate and GST carefully before you confirm.
