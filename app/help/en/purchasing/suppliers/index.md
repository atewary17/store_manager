# Suppliers

Everyone you buy from, and what you owe each of them.

## To add a supplier
Click **New Supplier** (top-right). You can also add one while entering a bill,
using **Add / Find Supplier**.

## What the columns mean
- **Name** — the supplier's business name.
- **GSTIN** / **PAN** — their tax numbers.
- **State** — decides whether their bills use CGST + SGST or IGST.
- **Invoices** — how many bills you have entered from them.
- **Total Purchased** — everything you have ever bought from them.
- **Outstanding** — what you still owe them right now.
- **Status** — whether the supplier is active.

Click any row to see that supplier's full history.

> Tip: if a supplier's **Outstanding** looks wrong, check **Supplier Payments** —
> a payment may not have been recorded yet.
