# Supplier Details

Everything about one supplier — their details, what you have bought, and what
you still owe.

## Supplier Details
- **Name**, **GSTIN**, **PAN**, **Address** — their business and tax details.
- **State** — where they are registered.
- **GST Type** — whether their bills use **CGST + SGST** (same state as you) or
  **IGST** (different state). This comes from the state above.
- **Status** — whether this supplier is still active.

## The money figures
- **Total Purchased** — everything you have ever bought from them.
- **Total Paid** — everything you have paid them.
- **Outstanding Payable** — what you still owe right now.
- **Last Invoice** — the date of their most recent bill.
- **Confirmed Invoices** — how many of their bills are finished and counted.
  Bills still in **Draft** are not included.

## The invoice list
Each of their bills, with **Date**, **Delivery**, **Amount**, **Paid** and
**Outstanding**. Click any bill to open it.

## To change these details
Use **Edit** to correct the name, GSTIN or state.

> Important: changing the **State** changes how GST is worked out on their
> future bills. Only change it if the supplier's registered state has genuinely
> changed.
