# Edit Supplier

Correct a supplier's details.

## Fields
- **Name** *(required)* — their business name.
- **GSTIN** — their 15-character GST number.
- **PAN** — their 10-character PAN.
- **State** and **State Code** — decides whether their bills use
  **CGST + SGST** (your state) or **IGST** (another state).
- **Address** — used on printed documents.

## What changing these affects
Corrections here apply to **future** bills. Bills you have already confirmed
keep the GST that was worked out at the time — they are not recalculated.

> Important: if you fix a wrong **State** or **GSTIN**, check any recent bills
> from this supplier. They may have been entered with the wrong GST split.
