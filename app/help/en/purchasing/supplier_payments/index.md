# Supplier Payments

Every payment you have made to a supplier.

## To record a payment
Click **New Payment**, then enter:

- **Supplier** — who you paid.
- **Invoice** — which bill the money is for.
- **Amount (₹)** — how much you paid. This can be part of a bill; the rest
  stays outstanding.
- **Date** — the day the money actually left your account.
- **Mode** — cash, cheque, bank transfer, UPI, NEFT or RTGS.
- **Reference** — cheque number, UPI reference or transaction ID. Useful
  later when matching against your bank statement.

## What the columns mean
- **Payment #** — the app's own number for this payment.
- **Invoice** — the bill it was paid against.
- **Recorded by** — which user entered it.

## What happens after you save
The bill's **Outstanding** drops by the amount you paid, and so does the
supplier's total in **Accounts Payable**.

> Tip: always fill in **Reference**. When you check your bank statement at
> month end, it is the only thing that tells two similar payments apart.
