# Payment Details

One payment you made to a supplier, and the bill it was paid against.

## Payment Details
- **Payment Date** — the day the money left your account.
- **Amount Paid** — how much this payment was for.
- **Payment Mode** — cash, cheque, bank transfer, UPI, NEFT or RTGS.
- **Notes** — the cheque number, UPI reference or transaction ID, if it was
  entered.

## The bill it belongs to
- **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date**, **Due Date** — which bill this paid.
- **Invoice Total** — the full bill including GST.
- **Paid ₹** — everything paid against that bill, including this payment.
- **Outstanding** — what is still owed on it.
- **OVERDUE** — shown when the due date has passed and money is still owed.

## All Payments on This Invoice
A bill can be paid in parts. This list shows every payment made against it, so
you can see the full history in one place.

> Tip: if **Outstanding** is not what you expect, check this list — a payment
> may have been recorded twice, or against the wrong bill.
