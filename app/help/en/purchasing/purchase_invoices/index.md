# Purchase Bills

Every supplier bill you have entered — typed in by hand or read from a scan.

## To add a bill
- **New Bill** (top-right) — type it in yourself.
- **Scan Invoice** — take a photo and let the app read it.

## What the columns mean
- **Invoice #** — the supplier's bill number.
- **Invoice Date** — the date printed on the bill.
- **Delivery Date** — when the goods reached your shop.
- **Total (₹)** — the full value of the bill, including GST.
- **Items** — how many product lines are on it.
- **Status** — see below.

## Status
- **Draft** — still being worked on. Stock has **not** changed.
- **Confirmed** — checked and saved. Stock **has** been updated.

## Buttons you may see
- **Confirm** — on a draft, checks the bill and adds the goods to stock.
- Click any row to open the bill and see the full details.

> Tip: a bill sitting in **Draft** has not added anything to your stock yet.
> If your stock looks low, check for unconfirmed drafts here.
