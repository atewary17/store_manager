# Bill Details

Everything on one supplier bill, and the payments made against it.

## What you see here
- **Invoice Details** — supplier, bill number, dates and who entered it.
- **Line Items** — each product, quantity, rate, GST and line total.
- **Payments** — money already paid against this bill.

## The money figures
- **Invoice Total** — the full bill, including GST.
- **Paid** — what you have paid so far.
- **Outstanding** — what is still owed.
- **Overdue** — shown when the payment due date has passed and money is
  still owed.

## Status
- **Draft** — not finished. Stock has not been updated. You can still edit it.
- **Confirmed** — finished and saved. Stock was updated at the time shown next
  to **Confirmed At**.

## To record a payment
Open **Supplier Payments**, or use the payment option on this bill, and enter
the amount, date and how you paid (cash, cheque, UPI, NEFT, RTGS or bank
transfer).

> Important: a confirmed bill cannot be edited or deleted, because the stock
> has already been updated from it. If something is wrong, speak to your
> System Administrator.
