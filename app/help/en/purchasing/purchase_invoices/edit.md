# Edit a Purchase Bill

Change a bill that is still a **Draft**.

## What you can change
Everything — supplier, invoice number, dates, and the item lines. Add rows with
**Add Row**, or remove a row you added by mistake.

Check the **Grand Total** against the paper bill after any change.

## Saving
Click **Update Invoice** to save your changes. Stock is still not touched.

To finish the bill, open it and click **Confirm** on the bill page.

## Why some bills cannot be edited
Only **Draft** bills can be changed. Once a bill is **Confirmed**, the goods
have already been added to your stock and the bill is locked.

If a confirmed bill is wrong, do not try to work around it by creating another
bill — that would count the same goods twice. Speak to your System
Administrator.

> Tip: fix mistakes while the bill is still a draft. It is far easier than
> correcting stock afterwards.
