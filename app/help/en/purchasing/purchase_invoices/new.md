# New Purchase Bill

Use this page to type in a supplier bill by hand.

> If you have a photo or PDF of the bill, **Scan Invoice** is faster — the app
> reads it for you.

## Step 1 — Choose the supplier
Start typing the supplier name and pick it from the list.

Not in the list? Click **Add / Find Supplier** and fill in the name, GSTIN,
PAN and state. The state matters — it decides whether the bill is charged
CGST + SGST (same state) or IGST (different state).

## Step 2 — Bill details
- **Invoice Number** — exactly as printed on the paper bill.
- **Invoice Date** — the date on the bill.
- **Delivery Date** — when the goods reached your shop.
- **Payment Due Date** — when the supplier expects payment. This is what
  **Accounts Payable** uses to show overdue bills.

## Step 3 — Add the items
Click **Add Row** for each product on the bill, then fill in:

- **Product** — start typing and pick from your catalogue.
- **Qty** — how many units or packs.
- **Rate** — price per unit, before GST.
- **GST %** — the tax rate for that product.
- **Amount** — the line total.

**Grand Total** at the bottom should match the total printed on the paper bill.
If it does not, something has been typed wrong.

## Step 4 — Save it
Click **Save Draft**. Your work is saved, but stock is *not* changed yet.

To finish the bill, open it from **Purchase Bills** and click **Confirm** on
the bill page. That is the step that **adds the goods to your stock**.

> Important: a confirmed bill cannot be edited or deleted. Compare your
> **Grand Total** against the paper bill before confirming.
