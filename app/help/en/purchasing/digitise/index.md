# Scan Supplier Bills

This page shows all the bills you have scanned, and what is happening with each one.

## To scan a new bill
Click **New Scan Invoice** (top-right) and follow the steps.

## What the words mean
- **Processing** — the app is reading the bill. Please wait; it updates on its own.
- **Review** — reading is done. Click the row to check and save it.
- **Confirmed** — you have checked it and saved it.
- **Failed** — the bill could not be read (blurry photo or wrong file).

## Buttons you may see
- **Open** — click a **Review** row to check the details.
- **Retry** — try reading a failed bill again.
- **Stop** — cancel a bill that is still being read.

> Tip: clear, bright photos read much better than dark or tilted ones.
