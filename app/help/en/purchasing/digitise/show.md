# Check and Save a Scanned Bill

The app has read your supplier's bill. Please check it is correct, then save it.
Your stock does **not** change until you save it.

## 1. Check each product
Every row is one product from the bill. If something is wrong, just type over it to fix it.

- **Green tick (&check; matched)** — this product is already in your product list.
- **Yellow "UNMATCHED"** — this is a new product. When you save, it goes to your
  manager's list to be approved. Your stock still updates.

## 2. Check the amounts
For each product the app works it out like this:

> Quantity &times; Rate &minus; Discount = Amount, then add GST = Total

- If a row turns **red (&#9888; ERR)**, the numbers do not add up.
- Fix the quantity, rate, discount, or total until the red goes away.

## 3. Remove a wrong row
Click the **&times;** at the end of a row to take that product off the bill.
Made a mistake? Click **Undo** on that row to bring it back.

## 4. The totals box (on the right)
This checks your rows against the total printed on the bill:

- **&check; All correct** — your rows match the bill total.
- **&#9888; Small difference** — a few paise off. Usually nothing to worry about.
- **&#10007; Does not match** — please check the quantities and rates again, or whether a page is missing.

## 5. Pages read
Shows how many pages of the bill were read — for example, "2 of 2". If a page is
missing, the total usually will not match.

## 6. Save
- Click **Confirm** to save the bill.
- Your stock updates once the bill is saved.

## If something looks wrong
- **"Off by &#8377;X"** — a quantity or rate is typed wrong, or a page is missing.
- **A product shows as new but you already have it** — type its name and pick it
  from the list, or check the supplier name at the top is correct.
- **GST looks wrong** — for known products the app uses the saved GST. You can
  change it on that row if you need to.
